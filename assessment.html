HelloEats is draft orientation, not a current task notification

Source materials describe a design challenge involving Australian food patterns, nutrition, influences and a ready-to-eat delivered meal. They conflict with the current handbook on task number, timing and outcome family, so this site does not invent formal conditions.

Safe to prepare now

  • Investigate food-consumption patterns and influences.
  • Compare nutritional evidence and Australian guidance.
  • Draft a safe, nutritious meal concept for a named consumer.
  • Record a bibliography and evaluate trade-offs.

Teacher to confirm

  • Whether HelloEats is the current assignment title.
  • Task number, issue date, due date and submission destination.
  • Marking criteria, permitted support and required file format.
  • Translation between the handbook’s older codes and the 2025 syllabus pathway.
